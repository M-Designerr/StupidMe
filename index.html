My love,

I wish I had been more thoughtful with my words. I made something that should have felt lovely feel different. I’m truly sorry. I understand that it hurt, and I don’t want to brush that aside or tell you how you should feel. Even though I didn’t mean to hurt you, I did, and that matters.

My intention was never to make you feel less special, or to make flowers feel like something you shouldn’t be excited about. I had been looking forward to giving you your first flowers from me because I wanted to see you smile. But I understand that what I meant and how it made you feel are not the same thing.

You don’t have to accept flowers from me. You don’t have to pretend this didn’t hurt, and you don’t owe me a happy reaction. I just want you to know that you are precious to me, and I’ll be more thoughtful with my words and actions. I hear you, and I’ll respect how you feel.

You deserve to feel cherished — not just through flowers, but in all the little ways, every day.

So, because you said you don’t need flowers from me, I asked these three tiny goblins to bring you a virtual bouquet instead. They took their job very seriously. One of them even practised the handover in front of a mirror.

They’re here to offer a tiny bit of sweetness, if you want it. They aren’t here to ask you to change your mind, and this page isn’t a request for you to be okay before you’re ready.

I love you. I want to show that by listening, choosing my words with care, and following through with my actions.

Whenever you want to tell me more about how this felt, I’m here to listen.

With love, and a promise to be more thoughtful ♡
